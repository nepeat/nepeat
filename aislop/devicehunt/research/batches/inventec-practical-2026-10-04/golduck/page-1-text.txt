5: Highlights
6: As an alternative performance server option based on 
7: 7nm AMD EPYC™ 7002 series processors (Rome), Golduck 
8: targets HPC (high performance computing), storage and 
9: general-purpose usage with 32 DIMM capacity  (up  to  4TB  
10: with 128GB DIMMs) and offers the expandability up to 2 
11: standard PCIe x16 cards and one OCP 3.0 card. Designed 
12: with a simple 1U enclosure, Golduck is optimized for large  
13: density and scalability with tool-less storage drives and 
14: hot-swappable fan modules. Perfect to various applications 
15: including  virtualization solutions and big data analysis 
16: scenarios requiring better flexibility, higher performance 
17: and greater storage capability. 
18: Boosted Performance and Computing Density
19: The dual AMD EPYC™ 7002 series processors provide up to 
20: 128 cores and 16 memory  channels for a single computing 
21: node.  With the industry-leading core count and the 
22: ground-up innovations, Golduck not only  offers ideal linear 
23: scalability and demanding virtualization capacity workload, 
24: but also boosts the performance of memory-intensive 
25: applications.
