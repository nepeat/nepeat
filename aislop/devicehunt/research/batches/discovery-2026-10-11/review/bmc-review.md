### Must-fix

### Should-fix
S1. `dts_psu_compatibles` mislabels non-PSU nodes (verified at source). (a) `nuvoton-npcm730-kudo.dts:590` is `pmbus@74 { compatible = "pmbus"; reg = <0x74>; }` under a `// STB-T` comment on an I2C mux channel; it is not shown to be a PSU, it was swept in by the `pmbus` compatible rule. (b) `aspeed-bmc-opp-zaius.dts:385,389` are nodes named `power-brick@6a` / `@30` with a comment "12V Quarter Brick DC/DC Converter Q54SJ12050 @6Ah / Q54SH12050 @30h"; DC/DC converters, not a PSU, and `delta,dps800` is the kernel binding string, not a part-number claim. The fact text says "may be unlabeled" and the handoff says "driver binding only", but the fact name `dts_psu_compatibles` and heading "PSU compatible strings" still reads as PSU evidence on a buyer-facing row. Fix: rename to something like `dts_psu_like_compatibles` with text "nodes matching name/compat heuristic; may be DC/DC converters, mux channel devices or sequencers", or drop the kudo `pmbus@74` and zaius power-brick entries (olympus `delta,dps800` power-supply@58, q71l/transformers psu nodes, and inspur power-supply nodes are named psu/power-supply and are fine). The q71l fact lists header `psu@58 | psu@59` for four cites, harmless.
S2. dts-side link fact wording. `dts_referenced_by_openbmc_machine` ("OpenBMC machine X: KERNEL_DEVICETREE expands to ...dtb ... expansion rule recorded on that machine record") states the derived relationship as if the machine "references" the dts. The conf never names the dts; it is derived by the stated expansion rule. The machine side says so; the dts side should too, and the fact name should say derived (for example `dts_openbmc_machine_by_expansion_rule`, text "derived, not a literal reference: ..."). Same for the handoff section title "In-repo links (stated, with expansion rule)" which should read "derived links (expansion rule), not literal references".
