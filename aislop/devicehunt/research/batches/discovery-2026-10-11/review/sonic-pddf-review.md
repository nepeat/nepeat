### Should-fix
S1. `pddf_plugin_psu_model_keys_in_fan_dir_map`: the fact text does not say the keys come from the `i2c` branch (the cited lines are inside `PSU.psu_fan_dir.i2c.valmap`, verified) and does not say it is a plugin lookup table of PSU model strings the plugin recognises, not a statement about shipped or installed PSUs. Add "i2c branch" and "lookup table in the plugin, not an installed PSU" to the fact text (and the handoff), since this is the field most likely to be read as "this switch has a Delta TDPS-1500AB PSU". The fact name's "model_keys" is acceptable.
S2. Handoff "NO conflicts" scope is narrower than it reads: it compared psus/drawers/fans/thermals only. Differences I found in the live catalog that the handoff does not list: silverstone_v2 `pddf_num_ports`=34 vs sonic interfaces/port_config rows 32 (and 128 for the 128X100G HWSKU); as7726 pddf 34 vs port_config rows 32; ds1000 `pddf_num_components`=1 vs 4 `sonic_component_names` (pddf-device.json:9 `"num_components":1`); ds3000 bmc `num_components`=10 while 9 COMPONENT entries are printed (:9 vs COMPONENT1-9); sonic ds3000 lists 10. They are different layers/units and not asserted equal, but state them in the handoff so nobody reads pddf_num_ports as a front-panel count.
S3. Handoff line "pddf_component_names (silverstone_v2, ds2000, ds3000, ds1000: BIOS etc.)" is wrong: only ds3000 has the fact (1 of 211; silverstone_v2, ds2000 and ds1000 have no COMPONENT entries in their pddf-device*.json, verified by grep). num_components exists for 4 platforms; names for 1. Correct the handoff.
S3b (locator precision, low). For `pddf_psu_devices`, `pddf_led_device_names` (nonbmc), and `pddf_fan_ctrl_devices`, the cited line is the object key / `dev_attr` line (e.g. `"PSU1":`, `"FANTRAY1_LED":`), while the printed value (device_name `PSU 1`, `FANTRAY_LED`, `num_fantrays`) is 1-2 lines below inside the same object. The values are correct against the object (verified via JSON), but the fact prints device_name (`FANTRAY_LED`) while the cited line shows the key (`FANTRAY1_LED`). Optional: cite the device_name line, or say "object starts at".
