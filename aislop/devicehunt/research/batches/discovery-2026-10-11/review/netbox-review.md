## Findings

### must-fix

### should-fix
- **S1. Module-type brand vs the accepted accessory convention.** The Edge-Core review (finding 3, adopted) moved PSU/fan order codes such as `YM-2651Y`, `DPS-850AB-5 A`, `UM400D01` and the FAN FRUs to brand `Unattributed accessory` so no brand claim attaches to third-party-looking PSU strings. The 11 NetBox module records instead carry brand `Edge-Core` (incl. `UPD1501SA-1179G` and `YM-2651Y B01R`). Result: after import there are two brand identities for the same strings (`Edge-Core FAN-1U-1x1I-F` and `Unattributed accessory FAN-1U-1x1I-F`, same for `-B`), and Edge-Core-brand PSU rows exist whose printed names appear in the Edge-Core datasheets as orderable FRUs. The role is not a manufacturer claim, but the brand column is. Suggest: for the 11 module records use brand `Unattributed accessory` with the existing `[Edgecore, listing_holder]` affiliation. Then FAN-1U-1x1I-F/-B would merge by exact brand+model with the datasheet FRU rows (additive, kind kept), and YM-2651Y keeps its distinct space-vs-comma strings. The Edge-Core 'all' inventory still sees them through the affiliation. Not blocking: counts and manufacturer scope do not change either way, and the integrator may instead accept the duplication as documented near-misses.
- **S2. Line-range convention for lists.** `netbox_interface_counts` and `netbox_module_bays` ranges end at the last entry's `- name:` line, not at its last property line. In all 22 interface files the final entry's `type:`/`mgmt_only:` lines lie after the cited end (e.g. `5912-54X-O-AC-F.yaml` cites 19-127 but the final `mgmt_only: true`, which supports "mgmt_only 1", is line 129; `7726-32X-O` cites 13-84, last entry runs to 85; `NF5180M6` module bays cite 16-28 but PCIe2 runs to 30). Counts and values are right; extend the end line to the last property line (build.py range end) and re-hash. Power-port ranges (first `- name:` to last property) and attribute_data ranges are right.
