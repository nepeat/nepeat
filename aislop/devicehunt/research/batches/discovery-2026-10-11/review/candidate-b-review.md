Should-fix (non-blocking, documentation or follow-up):

1. Brand-split duplicate identities (buyer-misread risk, medium). 52 Inspur/IEIT model families under 2+ brand strings, 7 `Quanta Cloud Technology` and 9 `QCT` same-model twins of `Quanta` devices, Sun/Oracle twins for 2 models (numbers above).
