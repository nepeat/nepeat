# AS7710/AS7760 certificate lead

Reviewed September 27, 2026. A newly located
[mirror of Master certificate MT-3593/2015](https://manuals.plus/m/964c2777a02dbbbdc1eaf9a3a484d06a52b2255cfba6e0c96b47e81171d2275c.pdf)
was readable as PDF text through the web tool. A subsequent direct original-byte
download returned HTTP 403. It was not retried or fetched through another route.
No original PDF was archived, no visual inspection is claimed, and this lead
does not add a database assertion. The archive still contains 33 PDFs.

Selected factual leads from the previously retrieved document text:

- Certificate model field names AS7710-32X and AS7760-32X; manufacturer field
  names Edge-Core Networks Corporation. Reissue date is March 30, 2016.
- Page 2 distinguishes Broadcom BCM56960B0KFSBG for AS7710-32X from
  BCM56930B0KFSBG for AS7760-32X. This does not establish interchangeability.
- The manufacturing-location field names Joy Technology (Shenzhen) Corporation.
  This is not an Accton factory mapping or evidence for a particular auction unit.
- The supply descriptions name YM-2651Y and FSF019. Extracted electrical ratings
  contain suspicious formatting; do not turn them into buying/power guidance
  without inspecting a reliable original or label.

Neither missing baseline label is established. The certificate names AS7760-32X,
not the complete 7760-32X-A-AC-F ordering code, and does not address AZ4444-52X.
Current validity and the identity of any individual unit remain unverified.

Renewed searches still returned complete 7760 ordering codes on seller pages
and AZ4444 variants or separately suffixed board identifiers. No new exact-label
manufacturer evidence was imported. FCC bulk-download searches principally
returned ULS station/license projects, which are not FCC device-ID databases;
these unrelated datasets were not downloaded.
