**Disposition: excluded from the acquisition plan at the user’s request.**

The user chose to disregard this provider and continue with our own collection
and open-source options. The review below is retained as history, not a pending
sample request or purchase recommendation.

Datadory advertises [FCC equipment-authorization data](https://datadory.com/datasets/communications-equipment/fcc-id-equipment-authorization-database-fccid-io-mirror)
delivered through files, API or a warehouse. Its page describes coverage from
1998 into August 2026 but provides no total FCC-ID count. The published field
dictionary lacks stable application and exhibit identifiers. Its descriptions
alternate between filing-level records and flattened FCC-ID observations;
preservation of multiple applications is therefore unverified.

The [search-interface description](https://datadory.com/datasets/communications-equipment/fcc-id-search-advanced-query-interface)
describes HTML scraping, not a public bulk-export endpoint.
[Pricing](https://datadory.com/pricing) is quoted on request and measured by
field/record/period observations. Samples require a form with contact details.
No contact, sample request or purchase was made.

Before this lead can close a coverage gap, obtain a sample with stable filing
and document keys, target-grantee counts, snapshot dates, explicit exclusions,
raw source links and local-retention terms. Test multiple applications under one
FCC ID and compare against known local records. Marketing scope is not evidence
of completeness or of physical manufacture.

All three public description pages were downloaded successfully and hashed in
`data/raw/fcc-bulk-review/review-bundle.json`. No device data was imported. The
working catalog and its verified rebuild remain unchanged.
