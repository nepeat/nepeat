- captures with `curl` into the worker's own raw dir: URL, HTTP status, UTC time and SHA-256 per
  file; a 402/403/429 is recorded once, never retried or rerouted; scripts live outside capture
  dirs and Python that reads captures runs with `-I`;
- candidate inputs use the existing `curated` JSON format (sources + devices + facts), never imported
  by workers; exact identifiers, suffixes and printed case preserved; company roles are not inferred;
- `devicehunt/review_queue.py` `projection()` / `_identity()` / `_content()` / raw-row extraction are
  frozen: the live campaign release pins their hashes (`test_projection_canary_is_frozen`);
