# Cross-brand factory leads requiring stronger access/evidence

Research on September 26, 2026 produced the following leads. None creates a
catalog assertion or factory match. Existing reviewed Cisco NCS1004/NCS1014
factory evidence remains in [the separate certificate import](cisco-celestica-factory-evidence.md).

- Cisco ASR-9000V-DC-E: a search-index excerpt for a conformity certificate
  labelled NCC 9743/13 lists Cisco as manufacturer and Celestica Thailand as
  factory. Public mirror URL:
  `https://fccid.io/ANATEL/02686-13-01086/Certificado-NCC-9743-13_Cisco/180E1E55-7C29-44B8-904E-D3AC32845626/PDF`.
  The web tool returned a cache miss; a normal direct request returned HTTP 403.
  Requests stopped, with no alternate access. The certificate has not been
  downloaded, visually inspected or independently associated with an original
  regulator/vendor publication. No factory relationship or electrical rating
  imported from the excerpt.
- Juniper EX4400-24X: an indexed compilation on Scribd mentions certificates
  JPTUV-143026 and JPTUV-143028, with Celestica sites listed on additional pages.
  No accessible original issuer/vendor copy was located in this pass. The
  compilation also includes other models; adjacency to SRX340 text is not a
  model/certificate association. Neither model nor factory information from this
  lead was imported. A general Juniper EX4400 datasheet does not establish the
  claimed factory relationship.
- Cisco MX100-HW: search again surfaced the already-known Cisco certificate URL
  ending `16616456.pdf`. Its earlier denied download was not retried.

The separate [Edgecore Chinese-name mapping](taiwan-target-evidence.md) did produce
verified new registry associations, using original government HTML and cached
registry bytes. Those applicant/manufacturer roles do not resolve these factory
leads.
