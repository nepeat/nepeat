mpeg2_cuda journey

An MPEG-2 encoder inside FFmpeg that runs entirely on the GPU, built in two days to feed 1080i transport streams into a Blonder Tongue EQAM for TVs at home. Every number below was measured; every milestone is a commit.

Frames per second, milestone by milestone

Each commit rebuilt from its own worktree and benchmarked back to back on an idle GPU: encoder only, 60 frames preloaded in GPU memory, 6000 frames per instance, best of two. Dashed lines are the CPU's mpeg2video on the same clip with matched settings.

Scope

Whole GPU = 4 encoder instances; whole CPU = 8 processes × 4 threads. On touchdown, milestone 8 jumps because progressive sources are now coded as progressive frames, which skips field motion search; interlaced park_joy stays put, as it should.

    Per-kernel time from Nsight Systems, park_joy 1080i, I/P.     On the air

    Six looping test channels (900 TESTCAT through 905 TSTFISH) run as NVDEC → mpeg2_cuda + AC-3 → MPEG-TS over UDP into the EQAM. A stress run of 6 channels × 4 copies held 24 live 1080i streams at 1.01× realtime with about 1.3% CPU; NVDEC, not the MPEG-2 encoder, is the ceiling for H.264 sources. The live senders were paused from 01:27 to 01:38 for the milestone benchmarks.

    Prior art: GPU pieces of MPEG-2 encoders appear in the literature (Montero et al., 2010–2013) and Elemental shipped closed GPU appliances; no open, fully on-GPU, decoder-bit-exact, interlace-aware MPEG-2 encoder was found. Details in docs/prior_art.md.
