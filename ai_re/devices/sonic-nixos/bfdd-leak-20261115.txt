## correction (19:58)
The "bfdsyncd stopped" isolation above was INVALID: sonic-bfdsyncd is UpheldBy
sonic-orchagent with Restart=always, so `systemctl stop` was undone at once.
malloc-trace on the switch (bfdd-malloc-trace-20261115.txt) shows the leak is
json-c objects built for a vty JSON show command (~5/s = bfdsyncd's 200 ms
poll) and never freed (allocs ~16600 per 30 min, frees 0).
