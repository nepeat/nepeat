## Dead ends (do not retry)
- teamd with "hwaddr" differing from the member netdev's MAC, or teamd -d with the team device set up
  right after it returns: port refresh fails ("Failed to find enabled option"), the port is dropped.
  Works: no hwaddr, foreground teamd, team devices up 2 s later (oracle/scripts/l2lag-teamd.sh). The
  same teamd works on veth with kernel 7.3.4 on the workstation.
- Two LACP ends on one box: both member netdevs carry the switch MAC; PortChannel2's member gets
  02:00:00:00:01:20 first (one system id per end).
- Linux bonding 802.3ad on the same netdevs (lacp-probe-20261116-052817 [96ec58ad]): no partner
  (Partner Mac 00:00:00:00:00:00, port state 79 vs 0); not pursued (SONiC uses teamd).
- Not testable on one loop: forwarding to a static FDB entry / LAG member choice (hash distribution),
  multi-member LAGs, both loop ports in one VLAN (storm).
