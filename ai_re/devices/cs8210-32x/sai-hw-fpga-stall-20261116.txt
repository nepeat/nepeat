CS8210 SONiC run 4 stall and I2C FPGA loss, 2026-11-16 08:10-08:36 (box time, PST)
Source: journalctl -b -1 on the box (boot ff215fd7...), lab VictoriaMetrics job cs8210-32x.
Captured 2026-11-16 by the SAI agent. Analysis: sonic.md "xcvrd and the I2C FPGA".

== Summary
* The I2C FPGA (0002:01:00.0, 1d0f:8571) answered at 08:10:29 (cage guard read the
  presence mask) and had stopped answering by ~08:10:35: from then on every access
  to it (BAR0 or config space) was a PCIe completion timeout.
* Such an access stalls the whole SoC, not only the reading CPU: scrape durations,
  fand's I2C (SoC controller) writes, exec of tiny scripts all timed out, and the
  hard-IRQ time rose evenly on all 16 CPUs, exactly while processes were reading
  the FPGA (08:10:3x-08:12:13: the four pmon daemons' Chassis() presence read;
  08:12:51-08:35: xcvrd's cage loop). 08:12:13-08:12:51, when nothing read it,
  the box was normal (scrape 0.12 s).
* xcvrd made it permanent: all-ones = 32 cages "present" (no "Transceiver not
  present" lines, 30 in run 3), so it polled EEPROMs of empty cages, one
  completion timeout per read (one empty cage's DOM update took 7 min).
  It used 46 min CPU in 25 min and had to be SIGKILLed; the guard's cs8210-qsfp
  afterwards spent 51 s on one read and failed.
* No kernel/AER line: external-PCIe INTx (AER) is not delivered on this kernel.
  IRQ 14 (19.7M) is the SoC I2C controller at its normal ~600/s, not the cause.
* Trigger not identified. Same sequence (guard read, Credo loader, four pmon
  daemons each reading the FPGA sideband registers at once without a lock) ran
  4 times earlier that morning without harm. Candidates: concurrent unserialised
  BAR reads from several CPUs (the vendor driver serialises all access), or a
  hardware event unrelated to software.

== Fix (2026-11-16)
* sonic_platform/fpga.py: every BAR0 access, sideband reads included, under
  cs8210-qsfp's flock (one access system-wide); sideband snapshot reused 0.25 s;
  before each session the root port's own config space (0002:00:00.0, on the SoC,
  never stalls) is checked: DLLLA clear, DevSta NonFatal/Fatal or AER CmpltTO ->
  /run/cs8210-qsfp.dead and no further access by any process; 0xffffffff from
  version/start/status/profile registers does the same.
* chassis.py: Chassis() reads nothing (psud, thermalctld, syseepromd never touch
  the FPGA; xcvrd is the one user).
* nix/nixos/module/cs8210/sonic: the cage guard checks the marker and the root
  port before calling cs8210-qsfp, retries while xcvrd holds the lock; xcvrd
  starts after the guard and the Credo bring-up.
* Tests: sonic_platform_test.py FpgaSafety (7 cases: no access from Chassis(),
  every fake BAR access under the flock with 4 threads, snapshot reuse, root port
  NonFatal / CmpltTO / link down, all-ones stops after 1 read, death mid-session).
