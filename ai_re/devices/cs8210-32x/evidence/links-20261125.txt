cage 2: -7.6 / -6.3 / -5.7 / -5.3 dBm, with every Tx at +2.2..+2.8 dBm (cage 1 lane 4 +0.5 dBm).
The modules' own thresholds: Rx power low alarm -8.9 dBm, low warning -6.9 dBm (show interfaces
transceiver eeprom -d). The same module type, application (400G-DR4, ApSel 1 on all host lanes, data
path Activated) and FEC on 31<->32 receive -1.8..-4.8 dBm and link at once. So 8-18 dB are lost in the
1<->2 fibre path: dirty / damaged MPO end faces or the wrong cable (polarity, APC vs UPC, MMF). Not
forced, not worked around: a human has to clean / swap the 1<->2 MPO cable (swap it with the 31<->32
cable to tell cable from module). Credo side configured as for optics (1:optics_1x400G, 2:optics_1x400G).
