== Sample frames (what reaches the CPU without a netlink): Ethernet 00:01:03:05:07:09 < 00:02:04:06:08:0a, IPv4
   0.0.0.0 > 0.0.0.0 TOS 0x08 TTL 100 proto 47, GRE 0x8818, 4 B, 16 B sFlow header, the sampled frame without FCS:
   58 B in front of it (a 256 B frame -> 314 B, 1500 B -> 1558 B: never truncated). Header word 0 = ext type 2 (sFlow),
   direction bit 15, ingress sysport (16 = Ethernet120); word 1 egress sysport (0: unknown at ingress); words 2 / 3 =
   the port's sample count and packet count since switch init (sFlow sample pool): consecutive samples' counters
   showed no sample lost between chip and CPU in any run (lost=0).
== The "1 in 64 for rate 100" oddity: the chip samples at random with mean 1 in N, but its generator restarts
   with the same seed at switch init and its gaps are clustered (gap cv 1.4-2.0; independent sampling gives ~1):
   the first 10000 packets after init at rate 100 give exactly 156 samples every time (qos-hw 054543 / 055312 and
   sflow-rate2 fresh enable #0: 156; the next 10000: 84, 110, 86). Over longer runs the mean is N:
   rate 100, 435491 packets: 4309 samples = 1 in 101.1 (10 runs at 1000-356000 pps, no send-rate effect);
   rate 256, 297822 packets: 1150 = 1 in 259.0; sweep 1 / 2 / 4 / 10 / 32 / 64: 1.0 / 2.0 / 4.1 / 10.2 / 31.9 / 65.8;
   1000 / 1024 / 4096: 1091 / 946 / 4225 (n 142-317, +-30 % at this dispersion); 64 B / 1500 B frames at 100: 98 / 119.
   So the configured N is the right rate to report (SAI's N is passed to IFCS unchanged, as isai does); short runs
   right after init over-sample. Rate 1 samples every frame (20000 / 20000).
== Stock ipd (no patch), SAI genetlink hostif psample/packets + SAMPLEPACKET trap + table entry (sflow-genl):
   IPD_SET_PARAMS (GRE 0x8818) + IPD_CREATE_NETLINK work in legacy_abi mode; ipd recognises the sample frames
   (LDH bit + GRE protocol), strips them and multicasts them on a family "psample" of its own: 494 messages for
   50000 frames at 100, iif = Ethernet120's ifindex, origsize 256 (1500 for 1500 B frames), group 1, and no sample
   reaches the netdev while the entry exists; samples on the netdev again after the entry is removed. Rate 0 in
   every message: the stock driver refuses INNO_ND_INGRESS_SFLOW_SAMPLE_RATE in legacy mode ("Operation not
   permitted", logged once by the SAI) and hsflowd drops samples with rate 0.
   Not understood: in that run the netdev also saw 0 samples before the table entry existed (step A; later runs
   and step H saw them). Not repeated with the patched driver (it is irrelevant once the netlink exists).
== Patched ipd (ipd-sflow-psample.patch, insmod'ed by hand: legacy_abi=1 psample=1), SONiC as booted (sonic.target)
   with this libsai through an LD_LIBRARY_PATH drop-in on sonic-syncd, sflowmgrd + port_index_mapper + hsflowd
   (sonic.sflow packages) as transient units, sflowtool on 127.0.0.1:6343 (tools/sflow-sonic-test.sh):
   - first insmod: CREATE_NETLINK returned -1 although the psample netlink was made (rc not set on the psample
     path): samples flowed with rate 0; fixed in the patch (rc = 0), re-tested below.
   - CONFIG_DB FEATURE|sflow enabled, SFLOW|global up, SFLOW_COLLECTOR c1 127.0.0.1:6343, SFLOW_SESSION|Ethernet120
     1000: coppmgrd/copporch made the GENETLINK hostif psample (ASIC_DB) + SAMPLEPACKET trap + table entry, sfloworch
     SAMPLEPACKET 1000 on Ethernet120 (400000 on the others, sflowmgrd's speed default); syncd: "sflow: samples to
     genetlink psample / packets".
   - kernel psample group 1, 300000 frames: 273 samples (1 in 1099), iif 114 = Ethernet120, rate 1000, origsize 256,
     proto 0x0800, group seq contiguous.
   - hsflowd needed sonic { waitReady=5 } (no sysmonitor sets STATE_DB SYSTEM_READY; mod_sonic waits 300 s
     otherwise; the module's default conf now has waitReady=10). Then 300000 frames: 336 psample messages ->
     sflowtool 336 FLOW records, all "in=121 out=0 size=260 rate=1000" (121 = PORT_INDEX_TABLE index of Ethernet120),
     agent 10.60.2.190 (enp0s1), plus 66 counter samples (ifIndex 1 / 121, 400 Gbit/s). Full decode:
     FLOWSAMPLE sourceId 0:121 meanSkipCount 1000 samplePool 337000 inputPort 121 sampledPacketSize 260 headerLen 128,
     inner 10.99.9.1 > 10.99.9.2 UDP 6343.
   - SFLOW_SESSION rate 1000 -> 100 live: 50000 frames, 499 psample messages with rate 100 -> 499 FLOW "rate=100".
   - Teardown: stock ipd back (modprobe), drop-in removed, SONiC as booted (Ethernet0/120 up), CONFIG_DB SFLOW*
     removed, transient units gone. port_index_mapper ignores SIGTERM (90 s stop timeout): TimeoutStopSec 5 in
     the module.
